ABOUT LIFELINK

Technology with
a human purpose.

LifeLink helps people discover volunteer blood donor profiles by blood group and city.

◎

Our goal

Make it easier to find community donor contacts through a directory organized by blood group and city.

⌘

How it works

Flask handles form submissions and searches. SQLite stores donor profiles and blood requests. HTML, CSS and JavaScript provide the interface.

♡

Responsible use

Profiles are not verified. Medical eligibility, compatibility and blood supply must always be confirmed by licensed professionals.
